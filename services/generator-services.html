Generator Services

POWER SOLUTIONS

Complete generator support designed to keep your home or business ready before the next outage.

Generator installation

Hurricane season is unpredictable—your power shouldn’t be. Eco-Tech Electrical Group provides professional generator installation to keep your property running safely and without interruption when outages occur.

Transfer switch installation (manual & automatic)

Ensure a safe and seamless transition to backup power with professional transfer switch installation. Eco-Tech Electrical Group installs both manual and automatic transfer switches, allowing your generator to power your property efficiently without risk to your system or the grid.

Utility coordination and permitting

We handle all utility coordination and permitting so your project runs smoothly from start to finish. Eco-Tech Electrical Group works directly with local authorities and utility companies to ensure your generator installation meets all codes and is approved without delays.
